2 · Seal → Log → Verify

Seal

cool.record() hashes the applicant data and the model's output, then discards the plaintext. It's signed twice — ML-DSA-65 (post-quantum) and Ed25519 (classical).

Log

The signed record is appended to an RFC 6962 transparency log. Editing any past record would break the root hash.
